Privacy Policy

Profit Pocket · Last updated 11 October 2026

Profit Pocket is a simple app for tracking sales, expenses and profit. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter (products, sales, expenses, recurring costs, projects, customers and any logos you add) is saved on your device. If you don't create an account, it never leaves your device.

If you create an account

Accounts are optional. If you sign in, we store your email address and a copy of your Profit Pocket data so it can be backed up and synced between your devices. This is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, and is protected so only your account can read it. We use your email only to sign you in.

Purchases

Premium is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether you have an active purchase.

Photos

If you choose a logo for a project, the picture you pick is shrunk to a small icon and saved with your data. The app doesn't access any other photos.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics or third-party tracking tools.
  • We don't sell or share your information with anyone.

Customer details you enter

If you record your own customers' names, emails or phone numbers, they're kept with your data as described above and used only to show them back to you.

Deleting your data

You can delete any record in the app. If you have an account, you can delete it at any time from Settings → Account → Delete account, which permanently removes your account and everything stored with it. Deleting the app removes the data on that device.

Children

Profit Pocket is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
